Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Who can apply to participate in the competition?
All students from TVET schools in Rwanda are eligible to apply. You must be enrolled in a public or private TVET school that is officially registered in Rwanda.
What can be considered a good project?
A good project is one that addresses a real-world problem and demonstrates innovation, feasibility, and impact. It should show creativity in solving challenges and have the potential for sustainable implementation.
How do I submit my project online?
Click "Apply Now" on this page, create your account, fill in your project details, upload supporting documents (PDF or images), and submit. You will receive a confirmation email with your application number.
What salaries or prizes do the winners receive?
Winners are awarded cash prizes, technology support, and 6 months of mentorship. The first-place project receives 500,000 RWF, the second-place 300,000 RWF, and the third-place 200,000 RWF..
Can I apply as a team?
Yes! Teams of 2–5 students are encouraged. All team members must be enrolled at the same TVET institution. One member acts as the project lead and primary contact.
When are the requirements returned?
The results will be released within 14 days after application. You will receive an email inviting you to proceed to the next stage. First-level winners will be invited to present their project before a panel of judges.
