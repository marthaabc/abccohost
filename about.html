Who We Are

About ABC CO

ABC CO is an NGO located in Gasabo District, Kigali, Rwanda. Our mission is to promote education — especially in TVET schools — through innovation, technology, and practical skills development. We believe every student has the potential to create solutions that matter.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

To support TVET students in developing innovative projects that solve real-life problems and sharpen their skills in technology, entrepreneurship, and creativity.

The Platform

What This System Does

Online Applications Students apply for innovation competitions entirely online — no paperwork needed.
Admin Management Administrators review, filter, and manage applications from one dashboard.
Real-Time Feedback Applicants receive live status updates and results as decisions are made.
Improved Communication Seamless messaging between students, schools, and organizers throughout the process.
Why It Matters

Benefits
